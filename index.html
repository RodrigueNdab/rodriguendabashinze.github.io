About Me

I am a researcher focused on bridging the gap between clinical trial activity and actual disease burden. My recent work examines the German healthcare landscape and the structural mismatches in research prioritization.

My vision is to utilize computational models and GBD data to inform better policy initiatives and pharmaceutical investments.

"How can we align clinical research with the true needs of the population?"

— My Research Philosophy
