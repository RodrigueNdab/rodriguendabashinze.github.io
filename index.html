Who I Am

About Me

RN

I am Dr. Rodrigue Ndabashinze, a medical doctor and MSc candidate in Global Health and Infectious Diseases at Moi University, Kenya. I hold my MD from the University of Burundi (2022).

My research focuses on infectious disease epidemiology, particularly malaria and neglected tropical diseases across sub-Saharan Africa. I integrate data science and machine learning methods to build predictive models that can translate clinical research into evidence-based public health policy.

Currently an ESI Fellow at Unity Health Toronto and a Research Project Assistant at the National Institute of Public Health, I am committed to bridging the gap between field epidemiology and advanced analytical methods.
