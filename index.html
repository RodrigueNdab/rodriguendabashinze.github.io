Who I Am

About Me

RN

I am Rodrigue Ndabashinze, a medical doctor (MD, University of Burundi, 2022) and epidemiologist (MSc Epidemiology, University of Antwerp, 2025 — Distinction) based in Rostock, Germany.

Currently I am a Data Manager for Clinical Trials Registries & AI at the University Medical Center Rostock (German Centre for Child and Adolescent Health, DZKJ), where I build NLP pipelines that map clinical trial health conditions across DRKS, ClinicalTrials.gov, and EUCTR to ICD-10 using hybrid ontology-embedding methods (SapBERT/FAISS).

I am also an Evidence Synthesis Ireland Fellow (University of Galway, placed at Unity Health Toronto), contributing to systematic reviews and health technology assessment. My MSc thesis focused on the transportability of effect measures in meta-analyses of randomised clinical trials across populations.

My research interests span causal inference (target trial emulation, AIPW, IOSW transportability), evidence synthesis, and AI/NLP for biomedical data.
